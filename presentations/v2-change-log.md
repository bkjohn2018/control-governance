# V2 Change Log

## Files Created

- `Finance_Accounting_Domain_Governance_Architecture_Deck_v2.pptx`
- `v2_change_log.md`

## Storyline Improvements

- Reframed the deck around a consulting-style executive narrative: problem, insight, architecture, AP proof point, and decision request.
- Added an early executive summary slide with the sequence: Govern the work -> Govern the analytics -> Govern AI use -> Bound agent activity.
- Preserved the core thesis that AI governance is downstream of Finance & Accounting domain governance.
- Preserved the core principle that AI and agents inherit constraints from governed operations and governed analytics.

## Slide Structure Changes

- Rebuilt the deck into a 14-slide executive reference architecture flow.
- Added conclusion-oriented slide headlines.
- Converted dense bullet slides into framework cards, comparison zones, decision gates, and compact tables.
- Strengthened the five-layer operating model with a Layer / Governance job / Primary question table.
- Added clearer treatment for Operations Architecture, Analytics Architecture, AI Augmentation Governance, Bounded Agents, Shared Governance Controls, AP as the worked example, and domain reuse.

## Visual Design Improvements

- Applied a consistent navy, slate, blue, teal, and purple palette.
- Increased white space and reduced body-text density.
- Added consistent headers, footers, section labels, cards, control backbone visuals, and domain chips.
- Used the finalized architecture image as the central reference visual.
- Used restrained callouts for implications, readiness gates, and AP worked-example framing.

## Key Wording Changes

- Replaced topic-style titles with executive headlines such as:
  - AI Governance Starts With Domain Governance
  - AI Risk Increases When Finance Work Is Not Governed as a Domain
  - AI Should Inherit Authority From Governed Operations and Governed Analytics
  - The Architecture Separates Domain Governance From AI Execution
  - Agents Should Be Approved Only After Readiness Is Proven
- Reinforced that AP is the first worked example and not the target architecture.
- Reinforced that Bounded Agents are optional and readiness-gated.
- Reinforced that Shared Governance Controls are the common control backbone.

## Assumptions Made

- The finalized PNG architecture visual remains the authoritative architecture graphic.
- The deck should be boardroom-ready but still usable by finance supervisors, directors, governance stakeholders, analytics stakeholders, and IT partners.
- The deck should avoid external statistics, unsupported claims, and AI hype language.
- The AP example should remain a proof point rather than an AP-specific implementation deck.
